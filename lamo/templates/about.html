ABOUT LAMO

Making Labour Management Simple & Reliable

LAMO is a web-based Labour Management System designed to connect employers with workers and simplify the complete process of finding, hiring, booking and managing labour.

WHAT IS LAMO?

One Platform for Labour Management

Finding the right worker for a job can often be difficult, especially when employers depend on informal contacts or local references.

At the same time, skilled workers may struggle to find suitable work opportunities.

LAMO brings both sides together through one digital platform.

One Connected Workforce

Employers and skilled workers connected through one platform.

Verified Worker Profiles

View worker information, skills and experience before making a booking.

Easy Job Booking

Create jobs, select workers and manage bookings through the platform.

Secure Management

Centralized records help employers and workers manage their activities efficiently.

Simple Dashboard

Important jobs, bookings and worker information can be managed from one place.

WHY LAMO?

Everything You Need to Manage Labour

A simple digital workflow for employers, workers and administrators.

Find Suitable Workers

Search and identify workers based on their skills, experience and job requirements.

Manage Jobs

Employers can create job requirements and manage their active and completed jobs.

Manage Bookings

Handle worker bookings and keep track of the current status of each job.

Attendance Tracking

Record and manage worker attendance for assigned jobs and work schedules.

Salary Management

Maintain salary and payment records for completed work and assigned workers.

Simple Dashboard

Get important job, booking, worker and attendance information from one centralized dashboard.

HOW IT WORKS

How to Use LAMO

LAMO makes finding and managing labour simple through a few easy steps.

01

Create an Account

Register on LAMO as an employer or worker and create your profile.

02

Find or Post a Job

Employers can post job requirements while workers can explore opportunities.

03

Connect & Book

Employers can select suitable workers and create bookings for the required work.

04

Complete the Work

Track attendance, manage the job and maintain records after completion.
