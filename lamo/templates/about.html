ABOUT LAMO

Making Service Booking Simple & Reliable

LAMO is a service-booking platform designed to connect customers with verified service workers and simplify the complete process of finding, booking and managing services.

WHAT IS LAMO?

One Platform for Service Bookings

Finding the right service worker for your needs can often be difficult, especially when you depend on informal contacts or local references.

At the same time, skilled service workers may struggle to find customers in their area.

LAMO brings both sides together through one digital platform.

One Connected Platform

Customers and skilled service workers connected through one platform.

Verified Worker Profiles

View worker information, skills and experience before making a booking.

Easy Job Booking

Create jobs, select workers and manage bookings through the platform.

Secure Management

Centralized records help customers and service workers manage their bookings efficiently.

Simple Dashboard

Important bookings, services and worker information can be managed from one place.

WHY LAMO?

Everything You Need to Book Services

A simple digital workflow for customers, service workers and administrators.

Find Suitable Workers

Search and identify service workers based on their skills, experience and service area.

Manage Services

Customers can browse service offerings and manage their active and completed service bookings.

Manage Bookings

Handle service bookings and keep track of the current status of each service.

Availability Tracking

Record and manage worker availability for assigned services and schedules.

Payment Records

Maintain payment records for completed services and bookings.

Simple Dashboard

Get important booking, service, worker and availability information from one centralized dashboard.

HOW IT WORKS

How to Use LAMO

LAMO makes finding and booking services simple through a few easy steps.

01

Create an Account

Register on LAMO as a customer or service worker and create your profile.

02

Find a Service

Customers can browse service offerings while workers can explore service requests.

03

Book a Service

Customers can select suitable workers and create bookings for the required service.

04

Complete the Service

Track service progress, manage the booking and leave a review after completion.

SIMPLE WORKFLOW

From Service Request to Completion

01

Service Request

02

Worker Selection

03

04

Service Delivery

05

Completion & Records

After completion, the system maintains relevant service and payment records.
