Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

LAMO is a service-booking platform connecting customers across Kerala with verified service workers for home repairs, electrical, plumbing, cleaning, and essential services. You select a service, choose an available date and time slot, and confirm your booking.

LAMO operates across all 14 districts in Kerala (Thiruvananthapuram, Kollam, Pathanamthitta, Alappuzha, Kottayam, Idukki, Ernakulam, Thrissur, Palakkad, Malappuram, Kozhikode, Wayanad, Kannur, and Kasaragod), with central support based in Kollam.

Every service worker registered on LAMO submits proof documents and undergoes administrative verification before they can accept customer bookings.

When a booking is confirmed, a secure 4-digit arrival OTP code is generated for the customer. When the worker arrives at the designated location, they enter this 4-digit code to formally start the job.

You can reach LAMO customer support by phone at +91 9496552094, or visit our office at 3rd Floor, Raj Plaza, Town Limit, Kollam, Kerala.
