COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

LAMO is a verified service-booking platform connecting customers across Kerala with background-checked service workers for home repairs, electrical, plumbing, cleaning, and essential maintenance. Simply select your service, enter your location, and connect directly with skilled local professionals.
LAMO operates across all 14 administrative districts of Kerala, with active local service clusters in Kollam, Thiruvananthapuram, Ernakulam, Kozhikode, and surrounding regions.
Every technician and service partner undergoes strict multi-step vetting, including government-issued identity proof verification (Aadhaar/PAN), trade skill assessment, and background screening before being approved on the platform.
To ensure maximum security and transparency, every confirmed booking generates a unique 4-digit arrival OTP in your customer panel. When the technician reaches your doorstep, they must enter this OTP to formally initiate the service session.
