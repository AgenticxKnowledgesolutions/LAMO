1 Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum personal information necessary to connect customers with skilled workers and deliver reliable services.

A. Customer Data:
  • Identity & Contact: Full name, verified email address, phone number.
  • Service Location: Physical address, city, district (e.g., Kollam, Kerala), and landmark details required for worker dispatch.
  • Booking History: Requested services, preferred schedules, notes, and recorded payment references.
B. Worker Data:
  • Professional Profile: Full name, trade skills, years of experience, hourly/service rates, service area radius.
  • Verification Documents: Identity documentation, government-issued IDs, trade licenses, and optional profile portrait photos.
  • Operational Metrics: Acceptance rate, completed bookings, ratings, customer reviews, and leave schedules.
C. Technical & Diagnostic Data:

IP addresses, browser type, device identifiers, and timestamped logs collected automatically to safeguard against brute-force attacks and rate-limit violations.

2 How We Use Your Data

Your information is strictly processed for legitimate operational and service delivery purposes:

  • Authentication & Security: Generating and delivering time-sensitive One-Time Passwords (OTP) and password reset tokens.
  • Service Coordination: Displaying the booking destination and customer contact details to the accepted worker, and vice versa.
  • Invoicing & Financial Accounting: Generating downloadable digital tax receipts and maintaining verified transaction trails.
  • Dispute Investigation: Reviewing messages, completion confirmations, and complaint notes in the event of an reported incident.

3 Lawful Basis for Processing

We process your data under the following legal frameworks:

  • Contractual Necessity: Processing required to fulfill service agreements between you, the worker, and LAMO.
  • User Consent: Explicit opt-in provided during account creation and OTP verification.
  • Legitimate Interests: Preventing spam, securing account endpoints, detecting fraudulent bookings, and complying with tax/statutory regulations.

4 Third-Party Data Sharing & Cloud Processors

Zero Data Selling Guarantee

LAMO never sells, rents, monetizes, or trades personal customer or worker data to third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.

We engage reputable, enterprise-grade cloud service providers who adhere to strict confidentiality contracts:

  • Cloudinary: Secure cloud storage for user profile photos and worker verification document uploads. Transmitted over encrypted TLS 1.3 connections.
  • Transactional Email (Resend / SMTP): Used solely to send transactional verification codes, booking confirmations, and password reset instructions.
  • Law Enforcement & Legal Compliance: We may disclose records if required by a valid court order, warrant, or governmental subpoena under applicable Indian law.

5 Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active and in good standing.

Right to Self-Deletion

You maintain the right to delete your LAMO account at any time via Account Settings > Delete Account (/profile/delete/). Upon account deletion, all personal identifiers, addresses, and active sessions are permanently erased from our production databases.

Anonymized transactional logs and tax invoices may be archived for statutory accounting and audit purposes as mandated by applicable tax statutes.

6 Security & Encryption Standards

We deploy industry-standard multi-layer security measures:

  • Password Hashing: Passwords are cryptographically hashed using PBKDF2 with SHA-256 and unique per-user salts. We never store plaintext passwords.
  • Transport Security: All traffic is encrypted in transit using SSL/TLS protocols with HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS) headers.
  • CSRF & Rate-Limiting: Multi-factor CSRF tokens protect all state-changing forms, and Redis/in-memory rate limiters prevent brute-force exploitation of authentication and contact endpoints.

7 Cookies & Session Management

LAMO uses functional and security cookies strictly essential for platform operation:

  • sessionid: Encrypted session cookie to keep you securely signed in.
  • csrftoken: Anti-forgery token protecting against unauthorized cross-site requests.

We do not employ invasive third-party cross-site advertising or behavioural tracking pixels.

8 Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under Indian and international data protection standards, you have the right to:

  • Access and obtain a copy of the personal data held about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete contact information via your Profile settings.
  • Withdraw consent and request erasure of your profile and data.
  • Lodge a grievance regarding the handling of your data with our designated officer.
