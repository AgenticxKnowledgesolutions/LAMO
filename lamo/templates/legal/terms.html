1 Platform Nature & Scope of Services

LAMO ("Platform", "we", "our", or "us") operates as an on-demand technology marketplace connecting customers seeking skilled labor, maintenance, and home repair services ("Customers") with independent tradespeople, technicians, and specialized service professionals ("Workers").

LAMO does not directly employ workers or deliver physical maintenance services. We act strictly as a communication, scheduling, and verified matching intermediary. All physical trade work is executed independently by the assigned Worker.

2 User Accounts, Authentication & Verification

To access booking or worker features, users must complete registration and email/OTP verification. You agree to:

  • Provide accurate, truthful, and up-to-date contact details, addresses, and phone numbers.
  • Maintain the confidentiality of your login credentials and one-time password (OTP) codes.
  • Workers must provide valid identification, skill credentials, and background documentation upon onboarding. Falsification of trade qualifications is grounds for permanent deactivation.
Multi-Role Notice

A single email address cannot hold both active Customer and Worker permissions simultaneously to preserve audit clarity and prevent transactional conflict of interest.

3 Service Bookings, Emergency Requests & Recurring Series

When submitting a booking request, the Customer specifies the preferred date, time window, address, and job requirements.

  • Standard Bookings: Confirmed upon worker acceptance. An estimated service cost is displayed on the booking details panel.
  • Emergency Requests: Dispatched with accelerated priority to nearby active workers. Additional surcharge or urgent minimum call-out fees may apply.
  • Recurring Booking Series: Automated periodic service bookings (weekly/monthly). Customers may pause, resume, or cancel active series at any time through the Series Dashboard.

4 Cancellation & Refund Policy

We maintain fair cancellation rules designed to respect both customer convenience and worker livelihood.

Customer Cancellations:
  • Before Worker Acceptance / Departure: Cancellations made while the booking is still in "REQUESTED" or "PENDING" status incur zero cancellation fee.
  • After Dispatch: If a booking is cancelled after a worker has been dispatched and is en route, a nominal nominal mobilization charge (call-out fee) may be assessed to reimburse the worker's travel expenses.
  • Emergency Requests: Due to immediate dispatch protocols, emergency bookings cancelled more than 10 minutes post-acceptance may be subject to standard travel compensation fees.
Worker or Platform Cancellations:

If an assigned Worker cancels due to unforeseen emergency, LAMO will automatically attempt reassignment to another verified worker. If no alternative worker is available, the booking is cancelled without penalty, and any advance payment is credited or refunded immediately.

Refund Processing Window

Approved refunds for verified disputes or cancellations are processed within 3 to 7 business days back to the original method of payment.

Independent Contractor Status: Service workers operating via LAMO are independent contractors and not agents, partners, or employees of LAMO. LAMO does not supervise the physical trade methods employed at customer premises.

Limitation of Liability

The worker performing the service is directly responsible for adhering to professional trade standards.

Customer Recourse: LAMO provides an integrated dispute resolution mechanism via the "Raise Complaint" feature. In the event of substandard workmanship or property damage, our administrative team reviews evidence submitted and may mediate between parties or suspend offending workers.

6 Code of Conduct, Non-Discrimination & Safety

All users (Customers and Workers alike) must maintain a safe, respectful, and harassment-free environment.

  • Workers must inspect work sites for hazards and follow standard electrical, plumbing, and structural safety codes.
  • Customers must provide safe access to the designated service area and disclose any known hazards (e.g., exposed wiring, gas leaks, structural fragility).
  • Zero tolerance for verbal abuse, harassment, discrimination, or unlawful conduct. Violations result in immediate ban and legal reporting where applicable.

7 Payment Terms & Invoicing

All service fees, material expenses, and applicable taxes are recorded against the booking.

  • Manual / Direct Settlements: When paying in cash or direct UPI, customers can record the manual payment transaction ID via the Booking Details screen for verification.
  • Digital Receipts: Once verified, an official tax-compliant PDF/HTML invoice is generated and accessible under the Customer and Worker dashboards.
  • No Cash Surcharges: Workers are prohibited from demanding unauthorized cash sums exceeding the agreed quote without submitting updated job requirements.

8 Reviews & Feedback Integrity

Customers can submit ratings and written reviews only following verified job completion. Each booking allows exactly one review to prevent duplicate or falsified feedback. Defamatory, fraudulent, or retaliatory reviews will be removed following administrative investigation.

9 Data Protection & Confidentiality
